About Pixel Radio

Pixel Radio is a pixel-art world radio. Drag the map, click a glowing station and you are listening to live radio from that place: tens of thousands of real stations, with the real weather where you are listening.

Turn on the radio

What you can do

  • Tune the world. Browse the map, search by name or by vibe (“hawaiian”, “upbeat cyberpunk”), or press Surprise me.
  • Moods. Pair a station with rain, a fireplace, a café or a storm, and make your own.
  • Focus. A Pomodoro timer, a sleep timer, and moods that start on a schedule.
  • Passport. Stamps for the countries you listen to, stats, and avatar gear you earn.
  • Friends. Add friends by code, see what they are tuned to and listen along, or study together in a room.
  • History. See what you listened to and save the songs stations announce.

Free, and calm

Pixel Radio has no ads and no tracking. The radio, map, moods, focus timer and passport are free. An account is optional; it only keeps your favorites, moods, avatar and passport in sync between devices.

Where the radio comes from

Stations are listed by the community-run Radio Browser directory and stream straight from the broadcasters. Pixel Radio doesn't own or control them, and a station can be off the air at any time. Weather comes from Open-Meteo, and the map is drawn from Natural Earth data. Fallback music is from Radio Paradise.

Who makes it
