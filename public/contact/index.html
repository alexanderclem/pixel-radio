Contact & support

Pixel Radio is made by one person, so email is the way in. I read everything and usually reply within a few days.

Email alexanderclem12@gmail.com

Helpful to include

  • A station won't play: the station's name (or the link from Copy station link), and your browser and device.
  • A bug: what you did, what you expected and what happened. A screenshot helps.
  • Your account or data: your Pixel Radio username (never your password). See the privacy policy for deleting an account.
  • Reporting someone: their nickname, the room link if there was one, and roughly when.

Station owners

Station listings come from Radio Browser. To add, fix or remove your station, edit it there and Pixel Radio picks up the change. If you'd like it hidden from Pixel Radio specifically, email me.
